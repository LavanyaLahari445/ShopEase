About ShopEase

A simple interface for product management and online shopping.

ShopEase is an online shopping system designed to bring product management and customer access together in one application.

Administrators can manage product information and inventory, while customers can access the customer portal to explore its available features.

Developed as a Java web application, ShopEase combines a clean interface with organized product management.
